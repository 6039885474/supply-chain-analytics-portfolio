## Last-Mile Delivery and Returns Analytics

**SQL, Python, SQLite, HTML/JavaScript | Synthetic data**

- Analyzed **5,457 synthetic shipments and 6,032 delivery-attempt events**, building interactive views of first-attempt success, delivery timeliness, return-to-origin, and attributable shipment costs.
- Reconciled **557 return cases** and separated completed-cycle measurements from open-return aging, identifying **18 unresolved cases older than seven days** for operational follow-up in the simulation.
